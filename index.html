A space designed for elegance

A landmark address for the moments that matter

Status Building Event Center brings a rare sense of occasion to Freetown. High ceilings, warm light and a graceful layout make it as fitting for a wedding reception as for a board-level conference. Our events team helps you shape the room around your day — from an intimate ceremony to a full banquet of 350.

Set within the gated Sierra Leone Grammar School compound, the hall offers generous on-site parking, room to breathe, and the quiet reassurance of a well-kept, secure venue.

  • ◆Flexible layouts — banquet, theatre, boardroom or lounge.
  • ◆On-site parking for up to 200 vehicles.
  • ◆Secure, gated grounds with managed access.
  • ◆Open vendor list — bring your own caterer and décor.
